What it does

A small, focused flow for fast photo selection.

Import selected photos

Start from Photos and review a selected set instead of scanning the full library by default.

Group similar shots on device

Bursts and near-duplicates are grouped locally so related frames are easier to review.

Start with a suggested best frame

Each group can open on a suggested frame so the first decision is faster, with full manual override.

Export winners to a Photos album

When you are done, Best Shot creates or reuses an album and adds your Keep picks in one place.

FAQ

Short answers for the release-stage product page.

Does Best Shot scan the whole photo library by default?

No. Best Shot is designed around explicit import, so you start from a selected set of photos rather than a default full-library scan.

Does Best Shot delete my photos?

No. The v1 workflow is export-only. Best Shot creates or updates a Photos album with your Keep picks and leaves the original photos untouched.

Do I need an account or cloud processing?

No. Best Shot is designed for on-device use and does not require account creation to review a session.

What does Best Shot export?

Best Shot exports the photos you marked Keep into a Photos album so the winner from each reviewed group is collected in one place.
