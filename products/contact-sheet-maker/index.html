Frequently asked questions

Can I preview before paying?

Yes. Import your photos, arrange them and preview your contact sheet for free. Pro is required to export.

How many photos can I select?

The app currently supports 6–36 photos per sheet.

Does the app change original photos?

No. It generates new outputs without overwriting your source photos.

What can Pro export?

Pro enables PDF and JPEG export, sharing, saving, and printing generated proof sheets.
